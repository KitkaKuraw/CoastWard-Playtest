# CoastWard

A cozy pixel-art colony builder. Sail to a new land, found a settlement and grow it from a handful of wooden huts into a thriving town: produce goods, keep your people happy, trade by sea and defend your coast. Play alone against computer opponents or with friends online.

🧪 This is a playtest version. Expect bugs and rough edges, and please tell us about them! Saves may not carry over to every future version.

What's in the game -
A new world every game: generated continents, rivers, mountains and climates, with fog of war to explore
Build and grow: about 40 buildings, production chains from wood and fish to cloth, rum and jewellery, and houses that move up through the tiers as their needs are met
Ships and trade: trade routes between your settlements, a free trader to buy from and sell to, villages inland linked by roads and rivers
Military: soldiers, cavalry, siege engines, warships, walls and towers. Defend against pirates or conquer rival towns
Computer opponents with their own personalities: builders, traders and warlords
Multiplayer: accounts, friends, lobbies, chat, diplomacy and trade between players
Difficulty from 1 (relaxed) to 5 (brutal), or set every option yourself
Optional tutorial for your first game, and an English, German and Czech version
Comfort features: undo, drag-building, blueprints, statistics and graphs, colour-blind modes, UI scaling and rebindable keys
Download & install
Get the files from the latest release (under "Assets").

🪟 Windows (10 and 11)
Download Coastward-Setup-<version>.exe and run it.
Windows will probably show "Windows protected your PC". The game isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows doesn't know it. Click More info → Run anyway.
Follow the installer. Coastward then appears in the Start menu.
No installing wanted? Use Coastward-<version>-portable.exe, which runs directly.

Updating: run the new setup; your saves stay. Uninstalling: "Uninstall Coastward" in the Start menu (or Windows Settings → Apps). It asks whether to delete your saves and settings too.
