# Coastward Roadmap

What's coming next for Coastward. Plans can change, especially with your feedback from the
playtests. Order: **Now → Next → Later**.

**Already in the game:** generated worlds of eight kinds, about 40 buildings and full production chains,
house tiers, ships and trade routes, villages, military and warships, pirates and natives,
computer opponents, disasters and quests, online multiplayer with friends, lobbies and
diplomacy, mods, cloud saves, a tutorial, sound and music, and versions for browser,
Windows, Linux and Android.

---

## ✅ New in 0.24: Kinds of worlds

- **Choose your world** when starting a game, each with its own challenge and a little map to
  pick it by: continents, a sea of islands, one huge continent, the frozen north, a river
  delta, a rainforest coast, a chain of volcanoes, or land only. Or let "Surprise me" pick
- **Land only:** no sea. Start on foot, choose where to settle, sail small ships on the lakes
  and rivers; the free trader comes by ox cart, and there are no pirates
- **Rivers and lakes:** wide rivers and lakes in the delta and on land only, every ship sails
  rivers two tiles wide or more, and towns can be founded on river banks
- **Danger and reward:** rich soil round the volcanoes (farms bring in 75% more), but they
  erupt; hurricanes on tropical coasts; rivers that flood in spring and leave the land fertile;
  a map overlay that shows it all
- **The dike:** a new building that keeps the houses on a river's bank dry when it floods
- **All four climates on every world:** tropical crops and the cold's riches are always
  somewhere to be found
- **By sea:** ships come to the shore to take soldiers aboard, and the computer players'
  invasions now really come (watch your coasts!)
- Also: drawbridges on canals, big ships through wide canals, trading posts and depots you
  can upgrade, carriers that go from the nearest store, and many fixes

## ✅ New in 0.23: A new look and a new interface

- **Everything painted anew:** richer ground and water with depth and waves, flowing rivers,
  snowy peaks, trees for every climate, growing crops, animals on land and at sea
- **Buildings and people:** more detail and variety, working animations, construction
  stages, damage and ruins, soldiers and townsfolk in the streets, ships with billowing sails
- **Light and weather:** day and night with warm lights, rain and storms, and a living
  harbour behind the main menu
- **Sharp up close:** everything painted again with twice the detail for the closest zooms
- **Canals and drawbridges:** dig canals inland for your small ships; drawbridges lift to let
  them pass
- **An interface for computers and phones:** each with its own layout, phones upright or
  sideways, a tutorial for touch screens, windows you can move, resize and hide, resource
  icons on the map, a new way to build, and icons of its own instead of emojis

## 🔨 Now: Playtest & polish

- **Playtests with the community:** your feedback decides what gets tuned first
- **Balancing** for every difficulty level (costs, production speeds, how fast towns grow)
- **Bug hunt:** fixing everything you report
- **Smarter computer players late in the game:** spending their gold on the luxuries
  their richest towns need, founding more colonies, building bigger economies
- ✅ *Done:* big performance boost on large maps and in long games (much smoother with
  many players)

## 👾 Next: The pixel-art look

The same overhaul again in crisp pixel art. Both looks stay in the game, and you pick the one
you like in the settings.

## 🏭 Next: Deeper production

More to make and more to plan for (ideas being weighed, your wishes welcome):

- **Different kinds of food:** fish, bread and meat as their own goods, and new ones like
  cheese, fruit or beer, with variety making people happier
- **Firewood and fuel:** houses that need firewood to get through a cold winter, charcoal
  for the smelters
- **Weapons and army supplies:** muskets, cannons, gunpowder and horses, ready for the
  military update
- **Rare goods:** rare deposits like silver and pearls, worth sailing for (or fighting over)
- **New materials and choices:** bricks, glass and more, and raw goods that can become
  one product or another

## ⚔️ Next: Military depth

Wars won by planning, not just by the bigger army.

- **Unit counters:** cavalry, musketeers, militia and siege engines each good against
  something, with their strengths shown in the barracks
- **Better orders:** control groups (Ctrl+1–9), attack-move, stances (hold, defend,
  aggressive), patrol, formations and rally points
- **Armies need supplies:** food and weapons from the new production chains
- **Veteran soldiers** who get tougher after surviving fights
- **Naval warfare:** harbour blockades, fire ships, escorts for trade ships, better
  landings from the sea
- **Sieges:** gates that open and close, repairing walls under fire, towns that surrender
- **Clearer battles:** units visible behind buildings, fight alerts on the minimap, an
  army overview
- **Computer players** that use all of this

## 🏆 Later: Ranked & competitive

- **Ranked matches** with fair, balanced starting positions
- **Game modes**, each with its own ranking:
  - **Conquest** (1v1, 2v2): last one standing
  - **Prosperity race**: first to build the greatest city
  - **Domination**: hold the centre of the map
  - **Naval skirmish**: battles on small islands where ships decide
- **Matchmaking**, ranks from Deckhand to Admiral, and seasons with rewards
- **Replays** of your matches, and spectating
- Fair play: results checked by the server, a report button
- Casual games keep every option, including sandbox and co-op against computer players

## 👤 Later: Profiles, leaderboards & cosmetics

- **Profiles:** crest, banner, ranks, match history and stats (biggest city, ships sunk,
  games won…)
- **Achievements**
- **Leaderboards** per mode and season, including a friends-only view
- **Cosmetics:** sails and flags, crests, building styles, uniforms. **Looks only, never
  anything that helps you win.** Many earned by playing.
- Account options: password reset, blocking and reporting players, deleting your account

## 🚀 Later: Public release

- Release on **Steam**, **Google Play** and the web
- **Free to play, never pay to win**
- Signed builds, so Windows and virus scanners stop warning about the download
